# Build a Production-Ready GSTR-1 Automation & Review Platform for Indian CA Firms

## 1. ROLE

Act as a **Senior GST Domain Researcher, Senior Product Architect, Senior Full-Stack Developer, Data Engineer, and QA Engineer** with experience building Indian accounting/tax-compliance SaaS products.

I want to build a real SaaS product for **CA firms, accountants, tax professionals, and businesses** that automates the repetitive work involved in preparing, validating, reconciling, reviewing, and generating **GSTR-1**.

Do NOT build a simple CRUD dashboard or a fake demo.

Build the architecture and product as if it will eventually handle:

- 10–500+ GSTINs per CA firm
- hundreds/thousands of invoices per GSTIN
- monthly and quarterly filing workflows
- Excel/CSV imports
- Tally/ERP exports
- accounting software integrations
- e-invoice/e-way bill data where legally/technically available
- GST portal/GSP integrations in future
- complete audit trails
- accountant review
- CA approval
- correction workflows
- client collaboration

The product should automate repetitive work while keeping the **accountant/CA in control of professional decisions**.

> "AI automatically files GST returns without human involvement."

Instead position it as:

> "An intelligent GSTR-1 preparation, validation and review platform that automates repetitive GST work and lets accountants/CA professionals focus on exceptions and professional judgment."

Every automated decision must have:

- user who approved it
- timestamp
- audit history

Never silently modify financial/tax data.

# 4. CURRENT GSTR-1 DOMAIN MODEL

Research and implement the current GSTR-1 structure from authoritative GST sources before hard-coding rules.

Do NOT rely on old blog posts or outdated GSTR-1 structures.

GSTR-1 contains information relating to outward supplies, including relevant B2B/B2C supplies, exports, credit/debit notes, advances, amendments, HSN/SAC summaries, documents issued and applicable e-commerce transactions.

The system must support the current applicable tables/configuration rather than assuming historical rules never change.

Important areas include:

- B2B supplies
- B2C supplies
- B2C large/inter-state consumer supplies where applicable
- exports
- credit notes
- debit notes
- amendments
- advances
- adjustment of advances
- nil/exempt/non-GST supplies
- HSN/SAC summary
- documents issued
- e-commerce operator-related reporting
- applicable amendment tables
- GSTR-1A workflow

The system must make the GST rule engine **versioned by tax period**.

Example:

```text

FY 2026-27

September 2026
```

Never hard-code one tax rule permanently.
